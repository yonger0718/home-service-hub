Notes for review

桌面 / iPad: ≥1024px = persistent two panes (list left, detail / entry / settings right), existing 72px dock, floating "+", keyboard shortcuts, amount field accepts expressions, no keypad. iPad portrait (760–1023px) = one pane; detail and entry slide in from the right as a sheet. Below 760px = the phone layout.

  • 紀錄 (home): month summary, day groups, foreign amounts inline (no tooltip), reward rows tagged 回饋, receivable rows tagged 應收 with 尚未收款 state.
  • 新增記錄: MOZE flow (kind tabs → category grid → amount + tiles → chips → note). Phone shows the calculator keypad with quick amounts; desktop gets a plain input with expression eval. Fee/discount and FX are bottom sheets.
  • 拆帳: the "+" after the selected category adds a line with its own kind, category, amount, account, counterparty.
  • 帳戶明細: passbook-style running balance above each amount (fixes the phone gap from phase 1). Period navigator follows the account's 帳單週期.
  • 帳戶設定: fields seeded from the MOZE backup. Credit toggle reveals card rows. Reward rules link opens the 2b rule list.
  • Tab bar: center "+" on phone, per decision Q3. Desktop keeps the dock plus a floating "+".
