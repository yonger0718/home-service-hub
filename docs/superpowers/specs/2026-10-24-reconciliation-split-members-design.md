# Reconciliation: split-group members printed apart — design

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: implemented on `feat/reconciliation-split-members`, see §9; rev 4.1 (AGENT-249 plan-review errata: two child views, adjust_amount records the group) — rev 4 folds the AGENT-248 dev-astra round 1 (rev 2: three majors, two minors) and the scoped re-check of rev 3 (the three majors re-raised: a scalar summary cannot reproduce the matcher's readings once pieces are covered). Rev 4 replaces the summary with a structural population and the matcher's own reading enumeration, failing closed where it cannot enumerate. Marks «AGENT-248 n» name the finding. Parent specs: `2026-10-08-statement-reconciliation-design.md` (§6 representations), `2026-10-15-reconciliation-r6-design.md` (R6a matcher rules, `printed_alone` 2026-10-19), `2026-10-21-reconciliation-inbox-design.md` (buttons, `match_candidate`).

## 1. Intent and why

The matcher reads a MOZE `split` group as **one purchase the owner split into parts, printed by the bank as one line**: a line is represented by the whole group (the net reading), by the group with its unclaimed children, or, since 2026-10-19, by the group minus the fee/reward members a fee line prints alone. A split member never stands alone for a purchase, withdrawal or deposit line (`_standalone`, `_lone_member`, test `test_a_split_member_never_stands_alone_for_a_purchase_line`), and the `match` action refuses a member (`entry_in_group`).

That reading is right for the dominant usage — 248 of the ledger's 337 split groups are **expense + receivable on one account** (a purchase for a friend: 跟朋友一起買, 待收) — and must stay. But the ledger also holds two shapes the matcher can never pair (triage 2026-10-24, `~/reports/home-hub-triage-20261023/triage.md` §Session 3; 23 of the 62 open `entry_unmatched` cases are members of 13 groups):

- **Cross-account splits** (38 groups ledger-wide, 6 open): one member on the card, the other on 錢包 / Line Bank / another card. The card's population holds one member → the group is *partial* → inadmissible; the member is not standalone; nothing represents the card's line. The sum never appears on any statement.
- **Same-card splits the bank prints apart** (open: 4316 and others): one line per member; no line equals the TWD or JPY sum.

A third shape explains several of the open expense+receivable groups and is **not** a matcher problem: the group's sum line exists but is held by a policy stand-in booked before the 2026-10-19 foreign-group fix (e.g. group 4302 ↔ line 1417, stand-in 58257; group 4343 ↔ line 3588, stand-in 28795). Those are resolved by `replace_stand_in`, and this design must not turn their members loose (§3).

**Owner decisions 2026-10-24:** make members participate individually; keep the group; a group whose sum the bank prints (expense + receivable) must keep matching as a group. Spec → plan → Multica.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

Goals:
1. A top-level member of a `split` group that the statement does not print whole is an **automatic** candidate like a standalone entry: exact representation (with its own children), foreign, near and drift cases, ambiguity, `claimed_by_earlier_line`.
2. The owner can `match` and `adjust_amount` such a member from the inbox, and the inbox's candidate search lists split members.
3. A group the statement prints whole — exactly, within the near tolerance, or by foreign sum; by any of its readings (net, with children, reduced by printed-alone members); **whether or not that line or any member is already covered** — keeps today's behaviour exactly (net reading, children, printed-alone fee members, foreign group sum, `group_readings_incomplete`).

Non-goals: new UI; changing group semantics in the ledger (no auto-dissolve, no regrouping); `reward_claim` and `installment` groups (unchanged: never members alone; `installment_group` path unchanged); a "several lines = one member" reading; R6c transfers; replacing the stand-ins of §1 (owner action, existing `replace_stand_in`).

Anti-goals (a failure even if the code works), scoped to **automatic** candidates — matcher claims, matcher cases, policy-applied proposals («AGENT-248 5»; the owner's explicit `match` and the inbox search are owner overrides, §5.1/§5.3): a member stealing or being listed against the group's own sum line or a line near it (the expense of an expense+receivable purchase must not become an `amount_delta` against the purchase line, nor claim a same-amount line elsewhere while its sum line sits covered by a stand-in or while a sibling is covered by an owner match); a member of a group printed whole appearing as a matcher candidate for an unrelated line; the `group_readings_incomplete` block bypassed by a member reading, in reconcile or in the policy validator; the policy auto-applying a proposal because the validator did not see a member competing; coverage rows that make a revert believe a whole group is claimed when one member is.

## 3. Definitions

- **Member**: a top-level (`parent_entry_id is None`) entry whose `group_id` names a group of kind `split`, under the instance rule of `_standalone` (no `installment` instance; a recurring instance is allowed — a split with a recurring member is inadmissible as a group today, so nothing is lost). Children of a member reach a line through the member's `member_children` variant or the child path, as for a standalone parent. A child of a **covered standalone** parent reaches a fee line through a carrier row — fee-child spec 2026-10-24.

- **Structural population** («AGENT-248 2», rev 4 — replaces rev 3's `GroupMember` summary, which the re-check showed cannot reproduce the matcher's readings). For every `split` group that has at least one member in the claimable population, `load_candidates` also loads **every other top-level member of that group** — whatever its account, currency, window, claim state or exclusion — as a full `matching.Entry` (`entry_view`, with **all** of its children as `Child` rows, claimed or not) marked `claimable=False` and `in_reverse=False`. A member that carries a schedule instance (recurring or installment) gets **no** structural row (Task 3 review ruling): the group then has a missing member, so it is whole by fail-closed exactly as when the instance member is claimable, and `_installment_case` never sees a structural row through `hint_only`. **Children are two views** («AGENT-249 1»): `Entry.children` stays the claimable view the loader builds today (participating account, statement currency, unclaimed); a new `Entry.all_children: tuple[Child, ...] = ()` carries **every child as stored** for every split-group member — claimable members included, whose covered or gated-out children the loader drops from `children` (reconciliation_service.py:215–220). The guard's children enumeration reads `all_children` (falling back to `children` when empty, for tests); claim paths never read `all_children`. These rows use the existing hint-only mechanism (`match()` already sets `hint_only` aside before the loop; `_drifted_instances` uses it): they are never represented, claimed, near/foreign-matched or reported unmatched, and `_group_variants` on the **claimable** list still finds them missing, so the claim-side partial rule is unchanged. The *guard* functions below take the unfiltered list (claimable ∪ structural). `Group` is unchanged.

- **Printed-alone pieces** (structural): `printed_alone(kind, lines_all, structural_entries, groups, rules)` — today's function, evaluated over every current line and every member (so a fee member excluded by owner coverage is still "printed alone" by its covered fee line, «AGENT-248 2»); plus **printed-alone children**: a child of a group member whose kind the line's gate admits and whose flow equals a line of `lines_all` outside `purchase`/`payment` (the child path of `_representations`, matching.py:380–387, over all current lines) — a covered discount line still marks its child («AGENT-248 1»). Both are over the guard lines (below) and feed the guard only (`aside_all`, `aside_children`); the claim side's `aside` is `printed_alone` over the claimable lines (§4.1).

- **Group readings for the guard**: exactly the readings `_representations` could build for the group on a fresh statement, enumerated with `claimed = ∅` over the structural population: for each member reading of `_group_variants(g, by_id_structural, ∅, aside_members, prune=False)` (the whole group and the group without each combination of printed-alone members, cap `ASIDE_COMBINATIONS_MAX`; **unpruned**: the claim side's subset-sum pruning compares net totals before children and runs with its own `aside ⊆ aside_all` and `claimed`, so a superset it drops can still be the claim side's `group_children` reading under a smaller `aside` — every claim-side member reading is one of the unpruned combinations over `aside_all`, and pruning or the claimed filter only remove readings; PR #130 review P1), the total without children and the total with the members' children **minus each combination of printed-alone children** (same cap; above it only "all children" and "no children" and the group is marked incomplete), **and the same enumeration over the claimable child view** (`Entry.children` minus each combination of its printed-alone children: the children the claim side would actually attach — a child covered on another statement is absent from it, so the reading the matcher would build is −480 while the structural all-children total is −280; execution findings 2026-10-24, Task 3 and its review) — so every reading `_representations` can build, in any line order, is a guard total. Foreign total per reading: Σ `original_amount` when every member of the reading shares one `original_currency` (`_foreign_groups` arithmetic). When `_group_variants` reports the group incomplete, yields no reading at all (a member carries a schedule instance — recurring included — or is missing, matching.py `_group_variants` first guard; execution ruling 2026-10-24, so a recurring-member split keeps today's behaviour and its members never go apart), the children enumeration is capped, or a structural row could not be loaded (defensive), the group is **whole by fail-closed** — its members never go apart («AGENT-248 1», "fail closed when the guard cannot enumerate").

- **Guard lines** (`lines_all`, final-review ruling 2026-10-24): every current line of this statement including lines held by owner/agent/policy coverage, **plus** the current lines of the participating accounts' other statements whose period intersects this statement's population window (`period_start − population_window_days` … `period_end + population_window_days`; a retired duplicate excluded; `reconciliation_service.guard_lines`). A group whose sum line sits on the previous or the next statement — including the §1 third shape, a sum line held by a stand-in there — is therefore whole here and its members, which the ±15-day population brings into this statement, never wander. `reconcile()` and the policy validator build the same set.

- **Mixed currencies** (final-review ruling): a reading whose members or children are not all in the statement's currency (a structural member on a foreign-currency account) cannot be summed with the line; such a group is whole by fail-closed.

- **Printed whole** (per statement, computed once before the line loop, over the guard lines): a split group with a reading whose total satisfies either
  (a) *amount*: the same amount (`_same`, so a 0 total against a 0 line counts) or same sign and `|line.flow − total| ≤ max(near_tolerance_abs, near_tolerance_pct · |total|)`, line within `max(exact_window_days, drift_window_days)` (15) of the reading's nearest member (`_date_distance`); the line's kind, gate and coverage are **not** consulted (a deposit, refund or fee line near an income split's total marks it whole; today no group reading exists for a non-expense gate, so without this an income split on a bank statement would always be apart); or
  (b) *foreign*: `|foreign total| == |line.foreign_amount|` (`_same`, magnitude), same currency, within `foreign_window_days`;
  or whole by fail-closed. Any reading the exact rules would claim today — net, with children, reduced by printed-alone members or children, in any line order and whether or not a piece is already covered — is whole under (a)/(b) because the enumeration is the matcher's own over the structural population.
  Amended by `2026-10-24-reconciliation-guard-holders-design.md` (#136): the guard's holders and their exactness rule are defined there.

- **Printed apart**: a split group in the population that is not printed whole. Its members are **apart members**. A cross-account group's total is known from its structural rows, so a card line equal to expense + receivable (receivable on another account) marks the group whole and nothing is stolen; a card line equal to the card's member alone leaves the group apart and that member is claimed. A member of an apart group that is covered on *another* statement (the 錢包 half matched on the 錢包 statement) is a structural row here and does not stop the card half.

The decision is per statement and per reconcile run; it is not stored.

Why "whole" is generous: the tolerance and window are the near and drift rules' own, so a group whose sum the bank prints a few TWD off (FX) or days later stays together and, as today, surfaces as the group's foreign `amount_delta` or stays open for the owner — never as a wandering member. The tolerance percentage is taken of the group total, while `_near_case` takes it of the line amount (reviewer note; equivalent at the tolerance edge within rounding). Covered lines and covered pieces are included so a sum line held by a stand-in or a fee member covered by an owner match keeps the group whole (§1 third shape): the owner's remedy there is `replace_stand_in` / `unmatch`, after which a reconcile pairs the group.

## 4. Matcher (`app/services/statements/matching.py`)

4.1 New pure functions, exported for the policy validator (§5.6):
- `printed_alone_children(kind, lines_all, entries_structural, groups) -> frozenset[int]` (child ids);
- `group_readings(g, by_id_structural, aside_members, aside_children) -> tuple[list[Reading], bool]` (`Reading(flow, foreign_amount | None, foreign_currency | None, members: tuple[Entry, ...])`, `complete`);
- `printed_whole(lines_all, entries_structural, groups, rules, aside_members, aside_children) -> frozenset[int]`;
- `apart_members(entries, groups, whole) -> frozenset[int]` (claimable members of apart groups, instance rule of §3);
- `statement_context(kind, lines, lines_all, entries_structural, groups, rules, account_currency) -> Context(aside, aside_all, aside_children, whole, apart, structural)` — `aside` is today's `printed_alone` over the statement's **claimable** lines (`lines`, owner-covered lines removed) and is what the claim loop and `incomplete_groups` use, so a fee line covered by another entry never changes which reading a purchase line claims (Goal 3, final-review ruling); `aside_all`/`aside_children` are over the guard lines and feed the guard only.

`match(statement_kind, lines, entries, groups, plan_map, fee_expected, rules, account_currency, lines_all=None)`: `lines_all` defaults to `lines`; `reconcile()` passes every current line (`all_lines`, before the owner-covered lines are removed from `line_rows`, reconciliation_service.py ~779–781) and the population including structural rows. `match()` computes `ctx = statement_context(...)` on the **unfiltered** list, then filters `claimable` as today, and threads the context:

- `_representations`: an apart member is admitted where a standalone entry is, with variants `member_alone` (rule as for standalone: `exact`, or the line kind's `payment`/`refund`/`transfer`) and `member_children` (rule `exact_children`) — rows `Row(e.id, "principal", e.flow, group_id=None)` plus child rows as for `principal_children`.
- `_foreign_case` single-entry branch, `_near_case`, `_drift_case`: `_standalone(e) or e.id in apart`.
- `_claimed_by_earlier_line`: same context (shadow representations).
- `incomplete_groups` («AGENT-248 3»): the loop's `truncated` check is evaluated over the **structural** population with `claimed = ∅` for the completeness flag (`ctx.structural`), with the claim-side `aside` (printed-alone members by the statement's claimable lines), so a group whose fee members were auto-claimed by this run or an earlier run still blocks the line (`group_readings_incomplete`), and a member reading never bypasses it. Fee lines the owner covered on this statement are not claimable lines, so they do not count towards the block — the group is still whole by fail-closed through `aside_all` (§7.13(b): a standalone twin may claim, the member never). It is still evaluated only when `reps` is non-empty (ruling on AGENT-249 Q3): an incomplete group is whole by fail-closed, so none of its members is apart and no member reading exists; a line with no reading at all keeps today's path. `_lone_member`, `printed_alone`, `_group_variants`, `_foreign_groups` unchanged.

`Row.group_id` stays `None` for member rows: a coverage row's `group_id` means "claimed by a group reading" (`_ambiguous` candidate shape, `match` params, revert closure of `touched_group_ids`). The sweep still finds the member's line through the snapshot's `group_id` (`lines_by_group`, coverage_service.py:369 and :501–503), so an update or delete of the group row releases the member's claim like any structural change — intended.

4.2 Competition. Apart members compete with standalone entries under the existing score/margin/ambiguity rules; a member and a standalone entry of the same amount and day are `ambiguous` as two standalones are.

4.3 Reverse side unchanged: an unclaimed claimable member is reported `entry_unmatched` (with `deferred_next_period`) as today; structural rows are `in_reverse=False` and never reported; `_designated_by_cases` unchanged.

4.4 `_ambiguous` keeps `{"entry_id": e, "group_id": None}` for member rows; group-reading candidates keep `{"entry_id": None, "group_id": g}`.

## 5. Actions, inbox, policy

5.1 `match` (`coverage_actions.py:113–121`): with `entry_id` naming a top-level member of a **`split`** group, the action proceeds like a standalone entry (role `principal`, `with_children` allowed, same `gate`, conservation required). `entry_in_group` stays for members of `installment` and `reward_claim` groups. The member's group is added explicitly to the lock targets and `touched_group_ids` (`lock_targets(..., group_ids=(entry.group_id,))`; today the group is locked only through `params.group_id`), so prepare's snapshot closure covers every member and a concurrent regroup is a `snapshot_conflict`. Coverage rows: `group_id=None` (§4.1). Proposal id scoping: a member's `entry_id` is an entry candidate like any other. Owner override: the action does **not** check "printed apart" — conservation and the owner's intent are the guard.

Revert semantics (reviewer note, folded into the plan's tests): the `after` snapshot records the touched entry and the touched group (`engine._after`, `snapshots.snapshot_set`) — the group row and its `member_ids`, not every sibling entry — so a later regroup is detected while a sibling's own later match is an ordinary later action touching the same group (`engine.py:433` dependency): reverting the first member match after a second one on the same group is refused `later_actions` as for any shared target. Plan tests: revert of a member match (restored to unclaimed, sibling untouched), two member matches on one group reverted in reverse order only, group update/delete sweeping the member's line.

5.2 `adjust_amount` (`ledger_actions.py:433–435`): the `not_standalone` refusal for `entry.group_id is not None` without `params.group_id` is relaxed to **split** members (other group kinds stay refused). The rewrite itself is unchanged (`apply` sets the fields and flushes; the dirty trigger and sweep handle the structural change), but `apply` records the member's group in `effect.touched_group_ids` (today the standalone branch :483–488 records none, «AGENT-249 3») while the coverage row keeps `group_id NULL`. The member's group is added to the lock targets as in §5.1 (today `lock_targets` takes the group only with `params.group_id`, :386–390). The foreign-group branch (`params.group_id`, 2026-10-19) is unchanged.

5.3 Inbox (`inbox_service.py`): `adjustable()` (:153) treats a split member as standalone (so `accept_bank_amount` is offered on a member's `amount_delta`); `search_candidates` (:234) includes top-level members of `split` groups (join `EntryGroup.kind == "split"`; still excludes other group kinds, children, claimed and listed entries). **Search is an owner override** («AGENT-248 5»): it may list a member of a group printed whole — the owner is looking for an entry by hand and the `match` that follows requires conservation; the automatic candidates (case `candidates`, buttons offered without a search) are the matcher's and never list such a member. The search payload marks members with `group_id` (already in `_candidate`'s source entry; add the field) so the sheet can show 「拆帳成員」. `_listed_candidates` unchanged. Buttons unchanged: `match_candidate` → `match` with `entry_id`.

5.4 `attach_fee`, `split_entry`, `convert_to_split`, `move_account`, `replace_stand_in`: unchanged.

5.5 Twin guard unchanged (`stand_ins.owner_twins` already counts members); `create_system_entry` is off in production (settings v15).

5.6 Policy validator (`policy_service._single_admissible`, :498–513, and `_is_proposed`, :484–490) («AGENT-248 2, 3»):
- loads the population exactly as `reconcile()` does — `rec.load_candidates(..., exclude=<this statement's owned coverage>)` (not every claimed entry: excluding this statement's own auto claims turns an auto-claimed member into a structural row whose child view differs from reconcile's — final-review Critical 2026-10-24) — builds the same `statement_context` with the same `lines` / guard lines, and passes `claimed=coverage_service.claimed_entry_ids(db)` to `_representations` over the claimable rows; passes `ctx.aside`/`ctx.apart` to `_representations` over the claimable rows, so a line reconcile saw as ambiguous between an apart member and a standalone has two admissible readings here too → `not_single_admissible`;
- refuses `not_single_admissible` when `incomplete_groups(kind, line, ctx.structural, groups, claimed=∅, ctx.aside)` is non-empty — the structural check of §4.1, so a purchase line blocked by reconcile stays blocked after the fee lines were auto-covered or the fee members left the claimable population by coverage on another statement («AGENT-248 3»); when the owner covers the fee lines here, `aside` (over the claimable lines) no longer counts them, the claim side has no group reading (the members are structural) and the guard keeps the group whole through `aside_all` — a standalone twin is then the one reading, as before the branch (final-review ruling, unit test §7.13);
- `_is_proposed` accepts `member_alone` for `entry_id` without `with_children` and `member_children` with it, beside `standalone`/`principal_children`.
No other policy change; the policy proposes no new action kinds.

## 6. Data migration / rollout

No schema change, no migration. Behaviour applies at the next `reconcile()` of a statement (manual, worker poll, dirty sweep). Rollout: deploy the API, then re-reconcile the statements holding the 13 open groups (121, 127, 128, 155, 156, 220, 224, 229, 504, 505) through `POST /accounts/{a}/statements/{s}/reconcile`; record before/after open-case counts in the release record. Expected production effect (triage 2026-10-24, not a promise): 4302 and 4343 stay whole (sum line held by a stand-in → owner `replace_stand_in`); 4316's members gain foreign cases; cross-account members (4260, 4280, 4282, 4300, 4319) pair with their own card line where one equals them; the rest stay open with a visible reason.
