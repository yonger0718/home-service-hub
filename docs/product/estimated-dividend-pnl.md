# 預估含息損益

The overview and each holding display price-only unrealized P&L + recorded dividends + eligible pending net cash dividends. The ratio divides this combined amount by the matching current cost basis, in the same currency, multiplied by 100. No positive denominator means no ratio. Incomplete valuation means the combined amount and ratio are unavailable, even when pending dividends are known.

`unrealized_pnl`, `total_unrealized_pnl`, their old percentages and `total_pnl_with_dividend` retain their existing contracts. New `estimated_pnl_with_dividends` / `estimated_pnl_percent` and native equivalents are explicit estimates. `pending_dividends_net` (summary: `total_pending_dividends_net`) uses the stored net amount, without recalculating quantity × rate or subtracting fees again.

Eligible pending rows are TW/TWD, have positive cash, are on or past their Taiwan ex-day, and have no review reason or source correction. Any same-symbol/TW-ex-day peer, regardless of status, or pre-existing linked cash leg excludes the pending event. Unresolved, future, stock-only and foreign pending rows do not contribute. Automatic rows must still match the recorded positive quantity under the existing pre-ex-day long, non-warrant eligibility rule. Post-ex-day purchases do not create rights; post-ex-day partial/full sales do not erase them. Manual/CSV rows remain supported entitlement declarations and do not require automatic position proof or imply confirmation. No persisted statuses are added.

When an unchanged pending amount is explicitly confirmed, it moves from pending to recorded. The combined estimate stays constant; the existing confirmation operation may post its single cash leg. Reading the summary performs no receipt, ledger, reconciliation or financial-state write. A stale automatic quantity is excluded until existing reconciliation resolves it; GET never repairs it.

The summary and TW market aggregate preserve all historical TW recorded dividends, including closed holdings. Pending closed entitlements are also retained at aggregate level, with no invented zero-quantity holding. Foreign recorded scope remains active holdings only. Holding identity is `(symbol, market)`. All uses TWD; market totals use TW/TWD or a single known native currency. New native estimates require matching cost/quote currencies and known dividend currency; mixed or mismatched units (including GBP costs versus GBp quotes) remain unavailable rather than silently mixing units. Existing foreign valuation and FX fields are unchanged.

This is the application's existing current-price-plus-recorded-dividend scope with pending added, not total lifetime trading profit. It does not change historical cash-flow XIRR, networth snapshots/charts, available cash, asset totals, taxes, fees, FIFO/cost accounting, receipt confirmation, list filtering or pagination. Legacy recorded amounts are not relabeled as proven receipts. Pending is included only in the explicitly estimated display, never available cash or cash-flow returns.
